The product

Wrist Hanzi shows Chinese vocabulary on your Apple Watch face and in a study session on iPhone and Watch. HSK 1 is free with no word cap. HSK 2–6 may be unlocked per book. A membership unlocks every book and the watch-face complication. The complication can also be purchased on its own. Words on the face come from unlocked books (HSK 1 counts). Short Chinese lines (Lines) come with the face add-on or membership and do not need a separate book.

Not an official exam

Word lists are arranged by HSK 3.0 levels for study convenience. Wrist Hanzi is not affiliated with Hanban / Chinese Testing International. Using the app does not enroll you in, or prepare you officially for, any exam.

Subscriptions and purchases

Prices are shown in the App Store. Auto-renewing subscriptions renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Restoring purchases uses your Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Watch face

You add Wrist Hanzi as a watch-face complication yourself. watchOS decides how often a complication actually refreshes. The interval you pick in the app is a request, not a guarantee.
