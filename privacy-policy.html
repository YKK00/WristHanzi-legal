Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Wrist Hanzi (“the app”) is an independent Chinese-vocabulary app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the app uses.

No account

Wrist Hanzi does not require a user account, email address, or social login. We do not run a profile database of learners.

What stays on your devices

Study progress, favorites, daily goals, pronunciation preference, and watch-face settings are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch. The app uses an App Group container so the Watch face can read the current word list. Pronunciation preference may also sync through iCloud Key-Value Storage if iCloud is enabled on the device.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. Wrist Hanzi receives purchase identifiers and entitlement status from StoreKit. We do not receive your Apple ID password, full payment details, or a name attached to the purchase beyond what Apple provides to the app.

Optional online entitlement check

If you use a paid feature, the app may contact a small entitlement service to confirm that a purchase has not been refunded or revoked. This check sends Apple transaction identifiers, not your vocabulary lists. If the device is offline for longer than the grace period, paid features pause until the check can complete.

Analytics and advertising

Wrist Hanzi does not include third-party advertising SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The app is a language tool and is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information about children through the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the App Store support contact listed on the Wrist Hanzi product page.
